# How to Have Deeper Conversations

Practical advice on how to get people to open up in conversations by prioritizing questions that invite stories rather than simple factual information, and by focusing on curious, active follow-ups.

## Concepts

- **Story vs. Information Questions** — The technique of shifting questions from factual data-gathering (e.g., 'What do you do for work?') to narrative-driven prompts (e.g., 'How did you end up doing that?').
- **Curious Follow-ups** — Focusing intensely on what the other person has just expressed and asking deep questions about it, rather than thinking ahead to find your own clever response.

## Evidence

- There is a fundamental difference between asking someone for information versus asking them for a story. (at 7.5s)
- Asking 'What do you miss most about where you grew up?' yields a story, whereas 'Where are you from?' only yields raw information. (at 22.0s)
- The best way to have deeper conversations is to ask better follow-up questions instead of searching for the next interesting topic to introduce. (at 43.5s)
