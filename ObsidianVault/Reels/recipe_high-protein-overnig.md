High Protein Overnight Oats (No Protein Powder)
Makes 1 serving per recipe | 3 recipes included
RECIPE 1: Carrot Cake Oats
½ cup (120g) almond milk
¾ cup (180g) 0% Greek yogurt
½ tsp (2g) vanilla extract
2 tbsp (20g) chia seeds
1 tbsp (21g) honey
¼ cup (28g) finely shredded carrot (packed)
⅜ tsp cinnamon
Pinch of nutmeg
Pinch of salt
½ cup (60g) quick rolled/steel cut oats
Macros (per serving): 535Cal, 31gP, 63gC, 12gF, 14g fibre (calculated using @cronometer_official app)
RECIPE 2: Pink Lemonade Oats
½ cup (120g) almond milk
¾ cup (180g) 0% Greek yogurt
½ tsp (2g) vanilla extract
2 tbsp (20g) chia seeds
2½ tsp (17g) honey
½ cup (70g) frozen raspberries
1 tsp (5g) fresh lemon juice
½ tsp lemon zest
Pinch of salt
½ cup (60g) quick rolled/steel cut oats
Macros (per serving): 550Cal, 31gP, 64gC, 12gF, 16g fibre (calculated using @cronometer_official app)
RECIPE 3: Funky Monkey Oats
½ cup (120g) almond milk
¾ cup (180g) 0% Greek yogurt
½ tsp (2g) vanilla extract
2 tbsp (20g) chia seeds
2½ tsp (17g) honey
1 tbsp (5g) cocoa powder
1 tbsp (7g) peanut butter powder
½ (~60g) medium ripe banana (mashed)
Pinch of salt
½ cup (60g) quick rolled/steel cut oats
Macros (per serving): 600Cal, 35gP, 73gC, 14gF, 17g fibre (calculated using @cronometer_official app)
Refrigerate in airtight containers for up to 7 days.
