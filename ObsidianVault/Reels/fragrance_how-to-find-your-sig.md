# How to Find Your Signature Scent Using the Fragrance Wheel

An educational guide explaining the fragrance wheel and classifying scents into five main families (Fresh, Amber, Floral, Woody, and Gourmand) to help you discover your unique signature scent.

## Concepts

- **Fragrance Wheel** — A classification tool used to define and map relationships between different scent families to help individuals select fragrances.
- **Fresh Fragrances** — Scents that feel clean, bright, and energizing, usually featuring citrus, water, green leaves, and fresh herbs like mint and basil.
- **Amber Fragrances** — A deep and rich family of scents typically featuring warm ingredients such as vanilla, spices, balsams, incense, and resins.
- **Floral Fragrances** — A highly popular fragrance family ranging from clean and soapy to soft, creamy, or sensual, utilized in both men's and women's perfumes.
- **Woody Fragrances** — Grounded, sophisticated, and earthy scents comprising notes like sandalwood, leather, vetiver, moss, or dry forest air.
- **Gourmand Fragrances** — Fragrances designed to smell edible, utilizing cozy, playful notes like caramel, chocolate, vanilla, coffee, and baked goods.
