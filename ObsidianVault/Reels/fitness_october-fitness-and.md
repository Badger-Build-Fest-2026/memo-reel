# October Fitness and Habit Challenge

A daily self-improvement and fitness challenge outlining nine rules to follow, including drinking only water, avoiding junk food, getting 7-8 hours of sleep, gym workouts, running, walking 10,000 steps, mindful eating without a phone, outdoor time, and practicing gratitude.

## Concepts

- **Water only** — Drinking exclusively water and avoiding sugary beverages, alcohol, and sodas.
- **No junk food** — Eliminating ultra-processed and unhealthy food options from the diet.
- **7-8 hours sleep** — Prioritizing adequate rest and sleep hygiene daily.
- **Gym 4x per week** — Engaging in four dedicated gym workouts weekly.
- **Run 3x per week** — Incorporating cardiovascular runs three times a week.
- **10k steps** — Achieving a baseline physical activity goal of 10,000 steps daily.
- **No phone during meals** — Promoting mindful eating habits by removing mobile screens at mealtimes.
- **20 min outside daily** — Spending daily time outdoors to get sunlight and fresh air.
- **Be grateful** — Maintaining a daily mindfulness or gratitude practice.
