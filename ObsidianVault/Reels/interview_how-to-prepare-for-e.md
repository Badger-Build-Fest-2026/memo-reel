# How to Prepare for Each Interview Stage

Joanna, a hiring manager and career coach, shares a whiteboard framework for preparing for recruiter, hiring manager, and peer interviews, focusing in-depth on how to pass peer interviews by aligning with company values and asking collaboration-centric questions.

## Concepts

- **STARL Framework** — A behavioural interview format standing for Situation, Task, Action, Result, and Learning, written under the Hiring Manager section of the whiteboard.
- **TMAY (P+P+F)** — A structured format for answering 'Tell Me About Yourself' using Present, Past, and Future components, recommended for the Hiring Manager stage.
- **Peer Interview Alignment Strategy** — A methodology involving researching 3-5 company values on their website, drafting examples demonstrating personal adaptability and team dynamics, and asking specific collaboration-focused questions.

## Evidence

- Joanna outlines a three-stage interview preparation process on a whiteboard covering Recruiter, Hiring Manager, and Peers. (at 0.0s)
- For peer interviews, Joanna highlights looking up company values on their website and identifying how you align with them. (at 24.0s)
- Joanna suggests preparing specific questions focused on collaboration, such as asking what good collaboration looks like in the team. (at 72.0s)
