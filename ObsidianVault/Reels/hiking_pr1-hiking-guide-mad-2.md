# PR1 Hiking Guide: Madeira's Ultimate Trail

An informative and scenic guide to hiking the PR1 trail in Madeira, showcasing spectacular mountain vistas above the clouds and recommending a sunset hike to avoid crowds.

## Evidence

- The PR1 trail in Madeira is rated as a 10/10 hike. (at 0.0s)
- The trail features steep, narrow stone steps with safety railings traversing ridge lines above thick cloud cover. (at 5.0s)
- Hiking the PR1 trail at sunset provides dramatic lighting over the ridges as the sun fades. (at 12.5s)
