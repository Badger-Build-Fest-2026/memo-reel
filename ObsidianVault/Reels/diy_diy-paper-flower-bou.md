# DIY Paper Flower Bouquet

A step-by-step tutorial showing how to craft a beautiful flower bouquet using simple materials like paper napkins, pipe cleaners, wooden sticks, yellow paint, and pink wrapping paper.

## Concepts

- **Tissue Paper Flower Folding** — An accordion-folding method used to create a multi-layered flower shape out of napkins or thin paper.
- **Pipe Cleaner Binding** — Securing the center of folded paper with a fuzzy wire stem (pipe cleaner) to keep the pleats in place.
- **Skewered Stems** — Attaching the paper flower base to thin wooden sticks to build a rigid stem for bouquet arrangement.

## Evidence

- The tutorial begins with a white paper napkin or tissue sheet. (at 1.5s)
- The paper is folded accordion-style back and forth. (at 3.0s)
- A green pipe cleaner is wrapped around the middle of the folded paper to secure it. (at 6.0s)
- The individual layers of paper are carefully separated and fluffed upward to form petals. (at 10.5s)
- Yellow paint is added to the center of the white flower to resemble pollen. (at 12.0s)
- The completed flower heads are mounted onto wooden skewers to serve as stems. (at 13.5s)
- The gathered flowers are wrapped in decorative pink paper and tied with a ribbon. (at 16.5s)
