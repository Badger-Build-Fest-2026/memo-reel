# Seed Mix for Hair Growth

A nutrient-rich seed blend featuring pumpkin, sunflower, sesame, and flax seeds designed to support thicker and fuller hair from the inside out.

## Evidence

- Pumpkin seeds are loaded with zinc which helps with hair growth and shedding. (at 6.0s)
- Sunflower seeds contain vitamin E and selenium for scalp health. (at 14.5s)
- The seed mix includes white sesame seeds, black sesame seeds, pumpkin seeds, sunflower seeds, and flaxseed. (at 31.5s)

> Original caption: The seeds I eat for thicker, fuller hair 🌱 because healthy hair really starts from the inside out, no product can replace what happens when you actually nourish your body. You can mix and match these however you like, but my go-to blend is pumpkin, sunflower, black sesame, white sesame, and flax.
A few tips:
🌱 Grind the flax so your body can actually absorb it
🌱 Toast everything to bring out the flavor
🌱 I’m showing a bigger batch here just for the visual, but you only need about a tablespoon of the mix a day, sprinkled on yogurt, salads, smoothies, or eaten as is
Just a heads up, this is a long game, hair grows slowly so give it at least 3-6 months of consistency. But these seeds nourish your skin, gut, and whole body along the way 🤍
