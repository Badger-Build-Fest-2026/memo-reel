# Essential Data Engineering and AI Concepts Explained Simply

An introduction to key concepts and terms separating AI talkers from AI builders, covering models, architectures, and semantic layers necessary for modern AI engineering.

## Concepts

- **Kimball Model** — A dimensional data modeling design containing a central fact table joined to surrounding dimension tables.
- **Medallion Architecture** — A data design pattern that logically organizes data into progressive refinement layers: Bronze (raw ingestion), Silver (cleaned/augmented), and Gold (business-level analytics ready).
- **Semantic Layer** — A middle layer between the data warehouse/lake and end-user business intelligence tools to translate complex data into readable, consistent terminology.
- **Lambda Architecture** — A data-processing architecture designed to handle massive quantities of data by using both real-time streaming (speed/hot path) and batch processing (batch/cold path).
- **Knowledge Distillation** — A technique in machine learning where knowledge from a large, complex model (teacher) is transferred to a smaller, faster model (student).

## Evidence

- The Kimball model consists of dimension tables surrounding a central fact table. (at 0.0s)
- Medallion architecture structures data quality into Bronze, Silver, and Gold tiers. (at 4.5s)

- Lambda architecture processes unified logs through hot/speed layers and cold/batch layers to feed serving views. (at 7.5s)
- Knowledge distillation is presented as a fundamental term separating people who talk about AI from those who build it. (at 9.0s)
