# Creamy Lemon Garlic Chicken Alfredo

A step-by-step recipe tutorial demonstrating how to make a creamy, delicious lemon garlic chicken alfredo pasta at home, featuring seared seasoned chicken breasts and a rich homemade cream sauce.

- Chicken breasts are seasoned on a cutting board with salt, pepper, garlic powder, paprika, and oregano. (at 7.5s)
- The seasoned chicken is placed into a hot pan to sear. (at 10.5s)
